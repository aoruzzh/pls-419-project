Methods & sources

  • Figure 1 applies the points reported in Section 2 to an archetype family of three with two earners and one child, at each per-person income band. Other indicators (car, housing, health) are held at zero because their values for this family are unknown.
  • Figure 2 multiplies 38 by the January MRP of each year and divides by the price level at the end of the previous year (December 2020 = 100), built from BNS December-on-December inflation.

Simulator assumptions

  • The demo uses the reported scoring points. The official methodology and Resolution No. 870 still need verification.
  • At overlapping C/D and D/E boundaries, the simulator shows both reported categories. The published material gives no numerical poverty line for Figure 1, so that portion shows a score range. Income band choices in the simulator keep both reported low-income bands.
  • The simulator applies the reported no-own-housing deduction when selected. Unpublished property, health, education, agriculture and other indicators are left out. The draft methods in the pack say housing is left out, while the build prompt explicitly includes that particular deduction.
  • The 2027 chart projection holds the 2026 nominal benefit constant and applies the selected inflation rate. The historical birth-benefit chart uses the figures supplied in the pack.
