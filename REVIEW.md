2. **Two exclusion mechanisms.** At 203,404 ₸ per person, three people have 610,212 ₸ a month. The one-tenge example illustrates a discontinuity for a middle-income family at the boundary. It cannot establish that poor families lose benefits there. Even two earners in the lowest positive band have only 40 + 300 = 340 points before deductions, below 730. Consider a second worked example with two earners and one child. Correct income gives 40 + 300 − 50 = 290 (C). A stale record placing that same family in the top band gives 620 + 300 − 50 = 870 (B). Distinguish inflated records from unrecorded informal earnings, which can bias estimated income in the other direction. Neither example measures the frequency of errors.

A sensitivity comparison would show why that choice matters.
